JIP numerical registration is derived from the JIP toolkit developed by Joseph B. Mandeville at Massachusetts General Hospital / Athinoula A. Martinos Center. Align incorporates a repaired/adapted native nonlinear engine inside its private bundled worker; it does not require a companion JIP application. Original MGH software-license terms remain at scientific/native/source/engine/JIP-LICENSE.txt. Modification provenance and validation boundaries are documented in docs/jip-changes.md. This is not an upstream JIP release.

The worker also incorporates ITK 5.4.6 (native B-spline registration and N4), FFTW, NIfTI C/znzlib, zlib, lmfit/MINPACK-derived code, NumPy, NiBabel and nlohmann/json. NiiVue supplies image rendering; Electron/Chromium/Node.js supply the desktop runtime. Original component licenses and notices are retained.
